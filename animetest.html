Quick FOQA

Is the library free to use? expand_more
Yes, AniClips is a free community-driven project. We do not charge for access to our standard or 4K/RAW library segments.
How do I download RAW clips? expand_more
Open any episode, click on a clip card, and use the "Download Ready" button. You can choose between 'Fast' (standard) or 'HQ' (RAW) depending on availability.
What about copyrights of the clips? expand_more
They are rightfully owned by the respective owners of the anime. We are just an editor community operate on principle of fair use and keeping the fanbase alive. We are industrially not recognised but tolerated as fan Media such as AMV, edit stimulates Blu-ray and merchandise sales. At no point do we monitise from this website nor do our users (you) as youtube claims all the money anyway, going straight to the rightful owners.
