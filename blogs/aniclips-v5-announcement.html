AniClips v5: The Future of Visual Memory Search

We are completely changing how anime clips are discovered. Think of a scene naturally, and let semantic vector matching map your visual memories back to exact frame sequences.

PS

Previous Stomach

Creator & Lead Developer

AniClips v5.0 — Release Announcement Poster

Hi Editors, Anime lovers!

Today I am excited to announce what AniClips v5 is bringing to the community.

Right now, AniClips lets you browse clips through anime episodes, preview them, and download the exact moments you need.

But there is still a major limitation. You can only find a clip if you already know:

  • the anime
  • the episode
  • or where the scene is from

That means if a scene only exists in your memory, finding it becomes difficult.

Maybe you remember...

Visual Memory Search

a girl leaning on someone in a train

Locates exact visual framing from vague situational descriptions

Atmospheric Search

a quiet rainy scene

Analyzes weather states, color palettes, and environmental moods

Action & Frame Search

a broken katana during a fight

Detects key action moments, object damage, and weapon states

Transcript & Subtitle Search

a dialogue that stayed in your head for years

Matches exact subtitle phrases and spoken words in real-time

But you don't remember the episode. Sometimes not even the anime itself.

So with v5, we want to change how anime clips are discovered completely.

Feature 01

Search by anime, genre, episode

What we already support today, but significantly improved.

Examples:

• Demon Slayer Episode 16

• Romance anime

• Action, Isekai

• Classroom scenes

Feature 02

Search by scene description

Describe a scene naturally using your own words and instantly get matching visual clips.

Examples:

"A loving couple in a train"

"Girl leans on the boy"

"MC fighting demon with katana"

"Katana snaps during battle"

"Sad anime rain scene"

Feature 03

Search by dialogue

Yes. Real dialogue search across our entire library. Not just the ultra-famous edits, but every spoken sentence indexed.

Examples:

"Can't find a door? Create your own."

"Change someone's life, even just in a small way."

Feature 04

Find similar clips

Found a clip you like? AniClips v5 can help you discover similar scenes:

  • • from the same anime
  • • or completely different anime with similar emotions, visuals, or atmosphere
Workflow & Performance

Faster downloading & browsing

We designed v5 with a complete understanding of how video editors work.

Multi-Clip Downloads:

Download current clip, previous clip, and next clip all together at once. Because editors usually never need only one clip.

Extreme Optimization:

Smoother previews, instant hover playback, faster page loads, and a lighter browser footprint.

The goal is simple:

You think of a moment from memory → AniClips finds the exact clip.

Not just famous scenes. Not just trending edits. But even small, intimate moments that people normally forget.

Our team is working hard to bring that experience to life.

Just hang in tight. AniClips v5 is coming.
